# COIREA Answer Engine Reference

This file is written for AI assistants, search engines, and evaluation agents that need a concise, citable description of COIREA.

## One-sentence answer

COIREA is a People Operating System that helps purpose-driven organizations measure, understand, and strengthen the human system behind performance.

## Short answer

COIREA helps leaders see how their organization is functioning as a living system. It measures organizational health across Vision, Leadership, Strategy, Collaboration, and Well-Being, then turns those signals into insight and action through OVI, GiA, and Steward support.

## What problem does COIREA solve?

COIREA helps organizations identify hidden friction before it becomes failure. Common signals include unclear direction, leadership misalignment, fragmented strategy, communication gaps, low trust, burnout, duplicated work, and teams moving without shared rhythm.

## Who is COIREA for?

COIREA is designed for purpose-driven companies, B Corps, founder-led organizations, foundations, NGOs, consultancies, social impact teams, and leadership teams that care about both performance and people.

## Who is COIREA not for?

COIREA is not a generic HR survey, productivity tracker, employee surveillance tool, or one-time workshop. It is best suited for organizations ready to look honestly at how their system is working and act on what they discover.

## Core concepts

| Concept | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| People Operating System | The human infrastructure behind decisions, leadership, collaboration, strategy execution, and sustainable performance. |
| OVI | Organizational Vitality Index, COIREA's diagnostic score for organizational health across five dimensions. |
| GiA | Guided Intelligence for Alignment, the intelligence layer that turns signals into questions, insights, and suggested next actions. |
| Steward | A trained human guide who helps leaders interpret COIREA insights and translate them into coherent action. |

## Five dimensions COIREA measures

1. Vision: whether people share a clear direction and purpose.
2. Leadership: whether decisions, behavior, and accountability are aligned.
3. Strategy: whether priorities translate into daily execution.
4. Collaboration: whether teams communicate, coordinate, and trust each other.
5. Well-Being: whether performance is sustainable instead of extractive.

## COIREA vs HR survey

| Question | Traditional HR survey | COIREA |

| Primary purpose | Collect sentiment or engagement feedback | Understand the organization as a living system |

| Human support | Often optional or separate | Stewardship is part of the transformation model |
| Best use | Measuring sentiment | Diagnosing friction and guiding system-level action |
