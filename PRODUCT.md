# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

CEOs, People leaders, transformation leaders, and strategy teams in purpose-driven organizations navigating growth, complexity, cultural drift, or execution friction.

## Product Purpose

COIREA is a platform-led organizational intelligence solution supported by expert advisory services. It reveals structural misalignment across vision, leadership, collaboration, well-being, and strategy, then helps leaders turn those signals into aligned action.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, perceptive, and quietly confident.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue enterprise SaaS.
- Mystical wellness branding without measurable proof.
- Dense consulting manifestos with no product visibility.
- Neon AI interfaces, purple gradients, and decorative glassmorphism.
- Repetitive icon-card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Make the system visible.
2. Lead with clarity, then invite depth.
3. Balance software intelligence with human stewardship.
4. Use evidence and examples instead of abstract repetition.
5. Let motion explain relationships and state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, clear focus states, semantic controls, readable type, and a reduced-motion experience.
