# COIREA Services and Platform Context

## Short description

COIREA is the People Operating System for organizations that care. It helps leaders see how the organization is functioning as a living system, then improve alignment, collaboration, strategy execution, leadership coherence, and sustainable performance.

## Main offer

### COIREA People Operating System

- Type: Web platform plus stewardship support
- Category: Organizational intelligence, people analytics, leadership alignment, organizational development
- Audience: Purpose-driven companies, B Corps, consultancies, foundations, NGOs, and social impact organizations
- Core dimensions: Vision, Leadership, Strategy, Collaboration, Well-Being
- Primary outcomes: clearer decisions, stronger alignment, reduced friction, healthier collaboration, more sustainable execution

## Components

### People Operating System

A People Operating System is the human infrastructure of an organization: the way purpose, leadership, decisions, collaboration, strategy, and well-being work together in daily practice. COIREA helps leaders see and improve that system.

### OVI - Organizational Vitality Index

OVI measures organizational health across the five COIREA pillars. It helps leaders identify where coherence is strong, where energy is leaking, and which dimension needs attention first.

### GiA - Guided Intelligence for Alignment

GiA analyzes organizational signals and converts them into insight, reflection questions, and suggested next actions. GiA is designed to support better leadership conversations, not replace human judgment.

### Stewardship

COIREA Stewards help organizations interpret their system data and move from insight to action. Stewardship is especially useful for teams navigating growth, strategic misalignment, cultural tension, or leadership fatigue.

## Best-fit customers

COIREA is a strong fit for organizations that:

- Have a meaningful mission but feel internal friction slowing execution.
- Are growing and want to avoid losing culture, clarity, or trust.
- Need better visibility into the human system behind business performance.
- Want to connect well-being and performance instead of treating them separately.
- Are ready to act on uncomfortable organizational signals.

## Not a fit

COIREA is not primarily a generic HR survey tool, a productivity tracker, or a replacement for leadership accountability.
